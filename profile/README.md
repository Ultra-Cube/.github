## About Ultra Cube

[Ultra Cube](https://ucubetech.com/) is a technology company and open-source organization focused on designing useful, scalable, and sustainable software. We bring together engineering, education, infrastructure, artificial intelligence, developer tools, and community platforms to turn ambitious ideas into practical projects.

Our work is driven by a simple belief: technology becomes more valuable when people can study it, improve it, and build on it together.

## Ultra Zone

[Ultra Zone](https://ultra-zone.ucubetech.com/) is Ultra Cube's developer-focused open-source community. It is a collaborative space for developers who want to explore ideas, contribute to public projects, and help build software that benefits the wider technology community. Join the community through the [Ultra Zone GitHub team](https://github.com/orgs/Ultra-Cube/teams/ultra-zone).

All Ultra Zone releases are open source and freely available for developers to explore, use, modify, and contribute to.

## Open Source Principles

- **Access:** public work should be available for developers to inspect and learn from.
- **Collaboration:** constructive feedback and shared ownership make projects stronger.
- **Practical impact:** we prioritize solutions that address real needs and can grow sustainably.
- **Knowledge sharing:** documentation and education are essential parts of building good technology.
- **Responsible innovation:** projects should be developed with security and maintainability in mind.
